A little preparation makes any consultation easier, for you and for the doctor.

## What to bring

- Earlier medical reports, test results and prescriptions, if you have them.
- A list of the medicines and supplements you take now.
- The name and phone number you would like the clinic to use for follow-up.

## What to think about beforehand

Write down the questions you want to ask. It is easy to forget them once you are in the room.
