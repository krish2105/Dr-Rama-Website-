Dr. Mathur keeps detailed clinical notes and records for each patient. Here is why that matters to you.

## Continuity

A written record means that what you told the clinic at one visit is still there at the next.

## Clear communication

Notes help the doctor explain things plainly and check them with you. Clear communication with patients is a part of how Dr. Mathur practises.

## Your information

The clinic collects only what it needs. You can ask to see, correct or delete the details you have shared. See the Privacy page for how to do this.
