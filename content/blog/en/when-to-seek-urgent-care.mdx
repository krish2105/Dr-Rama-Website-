## Specialist care

Dr. Rama Mathur refers patients to other health practitioners when appropriate. If a specialist has advised you on something, please follow that advice and tell the clinic about it.

## What to tell the clinic

- the medicines you take now,
- any reports or scans you have,
- any other treatment you are having.
