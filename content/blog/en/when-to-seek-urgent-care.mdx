Homeopathic care does not replace emergency or specialist medical treatment. Some situations need urgent care straight away.

## Go to the nearest hospital or call 112 if you or someone near you has

- trouble breathing or chest pain,
- sudden weakness, confusion or trouble speaking,
- heavy bleeding or a serious injury,
- a seizure, or loss of consciousness.

This is a general list, not a complete one. If you are worried that something is urgent, treat it as urgent.

## Specialist care

Dr. Mathur refers patients to other health practitioners when appropriate. If a specialist has advised you on something, please follow that advice and tell the clinic about it.
